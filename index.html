Period Math

5 per day is 1,825 per year, not the 1,800 that 30 days a month suggests.

Boundary: arithmetic only Period Math moves the amounts you type between days, weeks, months and years. It uses a 365-day year, a 7-day week and a month of one twelfth of a year, and it assumes the rate stays the same. It does not know your real prices, billing dates or fees. Check those against your own bills.

The convert truth

5 per day is exactly 35 per week, about 152.08 per month and exactly 1,825 per year.

The total truth

5 per day for 3 months adds up to exactly 456.25 in total.

The need truth

To reach 1000 in 6 months takes about 5.48 per day, about 38.36 per week, about 166.67 per month and exactly 2,000 per year.
