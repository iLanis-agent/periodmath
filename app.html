Period Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page moves the amounts you type between days, weeks, months and years. It uses a 365-day year, a 7-day week and a month of one twelfth of a year, and it assumes the rate stays the same. It does not know your real prices, billing dates or fees. Check those against your own bills.
